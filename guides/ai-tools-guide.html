Kiro Students（Kiro 学生福利）

UT Austin is on Kiro's eligible-school list. Verified students aged 18 or older can receive 1,000 credits each month for one year, without a credit card.

UT Austin 在 Kiro 支持的学校名单内。年满 18 岁并通过认证的学生可获一年内每月 1,000 额度，无需信用卡。

  1. Open Kiro Students and sign up or log in with Google, GitHub, or AWS Builder ID. Associate your university email with that account.
    打开官方学生页面，使用 Google、GitHub 或 AWS Builder ID 注册或登录，并关联学校邮箱。
  2. On the account usage page, choose verification in the student eligibility banner and complete SheerID verification. Enrollment documents may be requested.
    在账户用量页面的学生资格提示中开始 SheerID 认证，按要求提交在读材料。
  3. Check your account for activation. Unused monthly credits expire; after the year, the account returns to the free tier.
    在账户内确认福利生效。每月未用额度不结转，一年结束后账户转回免费套餐。

GitHub Education（GitHub 学生福利）

Applicants need a personal GitHub account, current enrollment in a degree- or diploma-granting program, proof of student status, and must be at least 13.

申请人须年满 13 岁、拥有个人 GitHub 账户，正在攻读学位或文凭，并提供在读证明。

  1. Add and verify your academic email when required by the application. Prepare a dated student ID, class schedule, transcript, or enrollment letter.
    根据申请要求添加并验证学校邮箱，准备带在读日期的学生证、课表、成绩单或在读证明。
  2. Open Education benefits, select “Start an application,” complete the form, and submit.
    打开教育福利设置，选择“Start an application”，填写并提交申请。
  3. Check your application status there. After approval, follow the Education portal's instructions to activate the available benefits and partner offers.
    在该页面查看申请状态。获批后按照教育门户指引启用可用福利与合作伙伴优惠。

Need an enrollment letter? See Texas One Stop: Verify Enrollment.
需要在读证明？请查看 Texas One Stop 的在读证明申请页面。
