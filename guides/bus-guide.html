UT Shuttle (校内班车)

UT operates its own shuttle system connecting campus, housing, and nearby areas. Routes, schedules, and live tracking are on the official UT Parking & Transportation site.

CapMetro App (市内公交)

UT students ride Capital Metro (CapMetro) buses and trains for free with a valid UT ID. Use the CapMetro app to plan trips, check real-time arrivals, and buy/validate fares (including MetroRail).
