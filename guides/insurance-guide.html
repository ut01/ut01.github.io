UT Health Insurance Guide 保险查询与豁免指南

Disclaimer (as of 202508): This is not an official UT document. Always verify with ISSS Insurance and UHS for the latest requirements.

Quick Answers 常见问题速查

"这个保险情况能在哪里查询啊" — Where to Check Your Insurance Status

What I Owe (Check Bar Status) ISSS Insurance Waiver Guidance Page UHS Insurance Page

For International Students (F-1/J-1)

Mandatory UT Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP) is automatically charged each semester. Check What I Owe → look for "Student Health Insurance" charge. If you have comparable coverage, apply for a waiver via the ISSS Insurance Waiver Guidance Page.

For Domestic Students

SHIP is optional. You can enroll voluntarily via UHS Insurance. If you have other coverage (parent's plan, marketplace, etc.), no action needed unless you want SHIP.

Check If You Have an Insurance Bar

Go to Registration Information Sheet (RIS). An "Insurance Bar" prevents registration. Clearing it requires either: (1) waiver approval, or (2) SHIP payment showing on What I Owe.

Insurance Waiver 豁免申请流程

Who Needs a Waiver?

  • International students (F-1/J-1) with comparable outside coverage
  • Students on employer-sponsored plans
  • Students covered under parent's plan (if meets UT requirements)

Waiver Requirements (Must Meet ALL):

  • Coverage valid for entire semester (Fall: Aug 15–Dec 31; Spring: Jan 1–May 31; Summer: Jun 1–Aug 14)
  • Minimum $100,000 medical benefits per condition
  • $50,000 medical evacuation & repatriation
  • Plan must be ACA-compliant (for F-1/J-1)
  • No exclusion for pre-existing conditions (for SHIP comparison)

How to Submit:

  1. click the link ISSS Insurance Waiver Guidance Page
  2. Select the correct reason you need a waiver (e.g. Graduate Student with an Academic Assignment (TA/GRA/AI))
  3. find the correct link Enter your insurance details (carrier, policy #, dates, benefits)
  4. Do as prompted
  5. Submit before deadline (typically 12th class day)
Go to ISSS Insurance Waiver Guidance Page

Key Deadlines 重要截止日期

Fall Semester

Waiver opens: ~June 1 | Waiver deadline: 12th class day (~early September)

Spring Semester

Waiver opens: ~November 1 | Waiver deadline: 12th class day (~early February)

Summer

Separate summer waiver if enrolled. Deadline: 4th class day of summer session.

Missed deadline? You must pay SHIP. Late waivers are rarely approved. Contact ISSS Advising immediately.

Common Issues & Troubleshooting

Waiver Denied?

Common reasons: coverage dates don't span full semester, benefits below minimums, plan not ACA-compliant, missing documents. Review denial email, fix gaps, and re-submit if before deadline.

Insurance Bar Not Clearing After Payment?

Payment on What I Owe may take 24-48 hours to reflect on RIS. If still showing after 2 business days, contact UT Service Desk or UHS Billing.

Dependents (F-2/J-2)?

Dependents are NOT automatically enrolled. They can voluntarily enroll in SHIP via UHS Insurance page. No waiver needed for dependents.

Graduating / Leaving UT?

SHIP coverage ends last day of semester. If graduating early, contact UHS to discuss coverage extension options (typically 30-60 days post-graduation).

UT SHIP (AHP/BCBSTX) Account Setup 保险账号注册

Per UT ISSS guidance for the UT Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP) administered by Academic HealthPlans (AHP) and Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas (BCBSTX):

  1. Create Academic HealthPlans (AHP) account: www3.academichealthplans.com/findCoverage
  2. Create Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas (BCBSTX) account using your Member ID
  3. Once both accounts are set up, use the BCBSTX App to view and use your digital insurance card
